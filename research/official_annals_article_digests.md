## 1783-06-03 《정조실록》 15권, 조재항 옥사
Official: https://sillok.history.go.kr/id/kva_10706003_004

- Article identifies 배천군, accused husband 조재항, his deceased wife 윤씨, distant kin and accuser 이가원(李可遠). Its prose places originating accusation in 1781 (辛丑), with repeated investigation over three years.
- The royal final assessment attacked 이가원 for trying to profit from his relation to the dead woman, inventing a village song to build public sentiment, later procuring added witnesses and instructing testimony in contradictory ways. The king cited incompatible testimony and the alleged bloodstained clothing having no trace.
- The king sharply criticized 도백 조상진 for delegating repeated reinvestigation and sending a superficial report apparently based on existing documents rather than conducting a new check. Official orders: release 조재항; exile 이가원 after corporal punishment; dismiss 조상진; lesser accomplices to be punished according to degree of fault.
- **Do not equate** royal narrative of 윤씨's death as a resolved medical autopsy. **Do not equate** wrong accuser with proven killer, or official negligence with proved joint conspiracy.
- Names appearing in the article useful for profiles: 조재항 趙載恒, 아내 윤씨 尹氏, 이가원 李可遠, 조재풍 趙載豊, 증인으로 언급된 배조이 裵召史, 나막동 羅莫同, 점렬 點烈, 막덕 莫德, 도백 조상진 趙尙鎭. Person disambiguation needed.

## 1787-04-16 《정조실록》 23권, 이곤수 보고
Official: https://sillok.history.go.kr/id/kva_11104016_003

- The article's **main** topic is the return and separate reports of 양서 암행어사 이곤수: administrative abuses, taxation, office selling, military obligations, etc. Do NOT treat unrelated institutional abuses in its paragraphs as proof that 최아지 case involved bribes.
- At the **end**, it separately records the unresolved 평산 case of deceased 박씨. 이곤수 was charged to conduct special inquiry and reported the hypothesis that her mother-in-law 최씨 and 조광진 had an illicit liaison and jointly killed her. Following the report 정조 ordered 전 도신 홍병찬 삭직 and 엄사만 파직 for failure to investigate diligently.
- This is the **1787 official conclusion**, later challenged by 1789 reexamination; a record of allegation and official finding, not a firsthand verification of exact killer identity. It does not by itself document the 1789 decision, precise defendant social status or the alleged five-nyang bribery.

## 1795-06-11 《정조실록》, 이여절·조진택 supervisory issue
Official: https://sillok.history.go.kr/id/kva_11906011_001

- This article records royal critique of the provincial inquiry's handling of allegations against 이여절 and dissatisfaction with the conduct of 조사관 and 관찰사 조진택. 
- Mechanism for coding: **administrative supervisory failure** and **official allegation that the existing inquiry shielded the subject** are supportable. A private agreement between two historical officials is **NOT automatically proven**. Record officials' criticism and actual inquiry orders separately.
- More granular victim counts, interrogation passages and final sanctions require separately supplied full text / later entries YE-004, YE-006, YE-007.

- Records final-stage royal discussion after 홍대협's separate inquiry into 구순's allegation and events leading to 김명신's detention and death. Preserve the multiple accounts and royal judgments rather than infer physical facts from any single testimony.
